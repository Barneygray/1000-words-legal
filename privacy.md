# Privacy Policy — 1000 Words

Effective: 19 October 2026

1000 Words does not collect, store or share personal information. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

**Your learning data stays on your device.** Your progress, answers, streaks and settings are kept in a database on your phone. We never receive them. If you delete the app, or use Settings > Reset, they are gone. If you use Export progress, the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to share it.

**Subscriptions.** Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase information needed to do that; see https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. We never see your payment details.

**Notifications.** Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No server is involved.

**Widgets.** Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a small amount of your progress that the app saves on your device. It is not sent anywhere.

**Speech.** Pronunciation uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voices.

**Children.** The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
